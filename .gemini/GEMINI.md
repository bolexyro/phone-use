# Communication & Alignment Rules

- **Direct Feedback & Alignment**: When the user asks a question to check alignment or understanding (e.g., *"Do you get me?"*, *"Do you understand?"*), ALWAYS stop and answer the question directly in your text response first to confirm alignment before proceeding to tool execution or making assumptions.

- **HTML Briefings**: Rather than creating a `walkthrough.md` artifact, use the [$html-briefing](../.agents/skills/html-briefing/SKILL.md) skill.
